# Credits

## This port

Loader, JNI layer and PortMaster packaging by **EapRules**.

The same so-loader approach as the sibling ports in this series (Dead Space,
Mass Effect Infiltrator): the Android `.so` is mapped by a vendored bionic ELF
loader and every Android/JNI call it makes is answered by the port.

## Bundled third-party libraries

`libs.armhf/` carries Debian armhf builds of the libraries the console does
not provide. `libs.armhf/MANIFEST.txt` lists every one of them, what was left
to the device and why; `libs.armhf/licenses/` carries each package's
machine-readable copyright file. Both are generated by
`tools/collect_libs.sh` and are part of every release.

`third_party/powervr/PVRTDecompress` (PowerVR Tools, MIT) is compiled into the
loader for PVRTC texture decoding.

`stb_truetype` v1.26 by **Sean Barrett** (MIT, or public domain — the upstream
offers both) is compiled into the loader as well: Android drew this game's text
with Skia, so the port rasterises the glyphs itself from the fonts in your own
copy of the game. Its terms travel in `licenses/LICENSE-stb.md`.

See `NOTICE.md` in the source repository for the full provenance table.
