Put your own Real Racing 3 2.7.0 (Android, armeabi-v7a) game files in this
directory. Nothing here is game data: the port ships only the loader.

Real Racing 3 PortMaster port by EapRules.

The launcher refuses to start until all three of these exist:

  lib/armeabi-v7a/libRealRacing3.so     (11150536 bytes in the 2.7.0 build)
  asset_list_base.txt
  assets_480x320/

Those come from a complete 2.7.0 install, not from the APK alone. The APK
carries the native library; the rest of the tree (asset_list_*.txt, the three
assets_* resolution buckets, Tracks/, vehicles/, gui/, audio/, eds/, ...) is
downloaded content and lives in the app's data directory on the phone. Copy
the whole thing here, flat, exactly as it is laid out on the device.

Roughly 3 GB. The SD card must be exFAT or ext4; FAT32 cannot hold it.

Do not place the PortMaster release zip here. That zip belongs in PortMaster's
autoinstall folder. See README.md for the complete installation instructions.
